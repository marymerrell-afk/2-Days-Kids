About 2 Days Kids

Preparing young people for what comes next.

Our work centers on practical life skills and the support young people need as they prepare for greater independence.

Our Purpose

2 Days Kids is focused on helping youth and young adults acquire, retain, and improve self-help, social, and adaptive skills.

These skills can support successful participation in areas such as employment, housing, education, and community life.

We aim to help young people approach their next chapter with practical tools, confidence, and meaningful support.

Our Mission
“Help us help them.”

The transition toward adulthood can require many different skills — from navigating everyday responsibilities to preparing for work, education, housing, and community life.

2 Days Kids is designed around those practical needs, helping participants develop skills that can contribute to greater self-sufficiency and participation in their communities.

What We Do
